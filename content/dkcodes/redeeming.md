## What a code can give

A code can give any mix of these:

- money, paid into your balance
- items, or a specific saved item
- experience points or levels
- a message, a title and a sound
- a server announcement

Some codes make you wait or pay: they can need a minimum amount of playtime, a particular world, a permission, or a cost taken from your balance. The code is only used up once everything checks out.

## Items that don't fit

If your inventory is full, the items that don't fit go to a mailbox. Type `/redeem claim` to get them. Rewards for players who were offline when they were earned, such as a referral owner's bonus, are delivered on their next join.

## Special codes

- **Player-only codes** can only be used by one named player.
- **Mystery codes** pick one reward pool at random, by weight, so a code can give a common prize most of the time and a rare one now and then.
- **Referral codes** belong to a player. When someone else uses the code, they get a reward and the owner gets one too. A player can only ever redeem **one** referral code, and you can't use a referral code from the same connection as its owner.

## Guessing codes doesn't work

There is a cooldown between every attempt, right or wrong, and too many wrong codes in a row locks you out of `/redeem` for a while. Staff are told when it happens.
