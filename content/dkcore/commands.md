dkCore has a single command, with one extra word to reload it.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/dkcore` | Shows the dkCore version, the database type, the economy provider and the bank provider. |
| `/dkcore reload` | Reloads `config.yml`. Database changes still need a restart. |
