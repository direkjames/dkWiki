This wiki explains the plugins that run on our Oneblock server: what they do, which commands they have, and which settings change how they behave.

Press `/` or **Ctrl K** anywhere to search. Search finds commands, settings and headings, so typing a command or a setting name takes you straight to it.

## Who each page is for

Every page has a label under its title.

- **Players** pages explain things you can do in game.
- **Staff** pages cover admin commands, permissions and settings.
- **Developers** pages are for people writing plugins that work with the suite.

## Good to know

> [!NOTE] Numbers here are defaults
> Tables show the values a plugin ships with. Our server may use different ones. When a page says to check something in game, that command shows what is really set.

Click any command on these pages, like `/bank interest`, to copy it.

If something here is wrong or out of date, use "Suggest a fix to this page" at the bottom of the page.
